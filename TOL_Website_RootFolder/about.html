About Tree of Life Foundation

Learn about our mission, vision, and the history behind our commitment to transforming lives through strategic giving.

Our Mission

To build a substantial endowment fund whose returns allow predictable, sustainable funding for qualifying charities, NGOs, and ministries in South Africa and across Africa.

We believe in transparency, impact, and the power of strategic giving to transform lives and communities for generations to come.

Our Vision

To multiply charitable impact over time by investing donations wisely, enabling donors to see their contributions grow to support long-term, strategic change.

Through strategic philanthropy, we aim to create a ripple effect of positive change that extends far beyond our immediate programs.

Our History

Tree of Life (TOL) was founded in 2007 in South Africa, based on a vision for sustainable giving rooted in Christian values.

By 2014, TOL adopted a progressive distribution policy, allocating a meaningful percentage of its net assets annually to vetted charities—a philosophy scaled through the DAF launch in 2016.

Today, we've facilitated over R5 million in donations to vetted organizations across 12 countries, impacting the lives of millions of people through education, healthcare, and community development initiatives.
