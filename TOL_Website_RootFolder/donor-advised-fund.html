Donor-Advised Funds

A simple, strategic way to manage your charitable giving and maximize your impact

How It Works

1

Make a Contribution

Make a tax-deductible donation to establish your donor-advised fund. You can contribute cash, securities, or other assets.

2

Receive Immediate Tax Benefits

Get an immediate tax deduction for your contribution, then recommend grants to charities over time.

3

Recommend Grants

Recommend grants to qualified charities whenever you're ready. Our team handles all the paperwork.

Benefits of a Donor-Advised Fund

  • ✓

    Tax Advantages

    Receive an immediate tax deduction for contributions to your fund, while supporting charities over time.

  • ✓

    Simplified Giving

    Consolidate your charitable giving with one tax receipt and one streamlined process.

  • ✓

    Strategic Impact

    Take time to research charities and make informed giving decisions that align with your values.

  • ✓

    Family Involvement

    Engage your family in charitable giving and create a legacy of philanthropy.
