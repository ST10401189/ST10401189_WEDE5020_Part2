Impact Stories

See how your support is transforming lives around the world

Transforming Education in Rural Communities

With support from Tree of Life donors, we've built 12 new schools and provided scholarships for over 500 students in remote areas of Cambodia. These initiatives have increased school attendance by 75% in partner communities.

"Thanks to the scholarship I received, I'm now the first person in my family to attend a private school. I'm studying to become a teacher so that one day I can give back to my community." - Sreyneath, Scholarship Recipient

Food Provision in Remote Regions

Our partnership with food provision Initiatives has established mobile kitchens serving 15,000 people across rural areas in South Africa and some parts of Kenya. The kitchens provide essential meals, sit downs, and emotional care services.

"The mobile kitchen saved my son's life as he was starting to fall ill easily due to malnutrition. This program was heavenly sent for sure." - James, Community Elder
